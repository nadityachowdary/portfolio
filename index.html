About

Curious by default. Data-driven by design.

I work at the intersection of machine learning and analytics. My projects range from deep-learning models that describe images and detect driver fatigue in real time, to SQL-backed analytics and automated ETL pipelines that make business data trustworthy.

I care about the whole path: clean data, honest evaluation, usable interfaces, and clear storytelling. Whether it's a webcam running at 30 FPS or a warehouse schema with referential integrity, the goal is the same — systems that hold up outside the notebook.
